# Otto Sensei: architecture and build spec

Single source of truth for everyone building this app. Read it fully before writing code.

## Product in one paragraph

A resume app with a friendly otter mascot, **Otto**, who guides the user. The user uploads an old resume (PDF or DOCX) and immediately sees the **recruiter view**: the exact text that hiring software (ATS) pulls out of the file, plus plain pass/warn/fail checks such as columns, icons, contact info in the header, and scanned PDFs. There is deliberately **no made-up 0-100 score**. Then Otto helps **fix weak lines**, asking for facts instead of inventing them ("How many users?"). Next the user can **tailor** the resume to one job from its link (or pasted text when the link can't be read): missing keywords they really have get added, and bullets are reordered. Finally they **download a clean PDF, free, always**, and we re-check our own PDF to show "before: 4 problems, after: 0". **Free basics + Pro trial** (decided 2026-11-08): Free, with no card and no time limit, keeps only uploads read by the rule-based reader, the recruiter view, editing and fixing by hand, **1 resume** (plus the sample, which has a slot of its own in `saveBackup`), the **Classic and Compact** formats and PDF download, and has **no AI allowance at all** (owner decision 2026-11-08). The **Pro tools** (`ProTool` in `src/config/plans.ts`: up to 50 resumes, the other four formats, Word download, job matching and saved jobs, the tracker and its reminders, share links, the work-rights check) need Pro, Max or the trial. Every AI feature (line fixes, summaries, tailoring, letters, answers, interview prep, Build with Otto, GitHub drafts, AI reads of uploads) and the portfolio need Pro or Max. New accounts can start Pro on a **$1, 3-day trial, card required** (owner decision 2026-11-18: a separate Dodo product, Pro monthly with `trial_period_days` 3 and `trial_amount` 100, since Dodo sets a paid trial per product, not per checkout; Dodo charges `PRO_TRIAL.priceUsd` plus tax at checkout and always asks for a card), then $15/month; one trial per person (`billing_log` kind `trial`, by email hash). The trial has its own allowance for the whole trial (`PRO_TRIAL`): 10 tailored versions, 15 letters/answers/prep, 50 line fixes, 5 AI reads, 3 Build with Otto, 3 GitHub drafts (worst case about $0.42 of AI per trial, about covered by the $1, which nets about 55¢ after Dodo's 4% + 0.5% for subscriptions + 40¢). Paid limits (`src/config/plans.ts`), per billing month (a yearly plan counts monthly from the day it started), in the same order: Pro ($15/month or $120/year) 150/150/750/100/50/50; Max ($29/month or $232/year) 300/300/2000/300/150/150. Plans bill monthly or yearly; there are no weekly plans (owner decision 2026-11-14). Uploads are never blocked: on Free, signed out, or past the AI reads, the rule-based reader takes over. Every upload is read by rules first; the AI reader (and one AI read) is used only when that read looks weak (`src/lib/resume/read-quality.ts`: PDF columns, Word text boxes, no jobs, an empty section, a nameless or lineless job, or headings, an email or job dates in the summary), or when Pro asks for it with "Let Otto re-read it" (`POST /api/import?reader=ai`).

Around that core flow: a **Jobs** tab of real openings from public company job boards and two remote job sites, matched to the resume by rules (no AI; Pro, with a count-only preview on Free), each one click from a tailored copy and the tracker (there is no public jobs page since 2026-11-11: `/jobs` and `/jobs/*` redirect to the home page's Jobs section); undo/redo, saved versions and a trash; extra sections (languages, awards, volunteering, publications, custom); a look panel, fit-to-one-page, Word export and a send check; Otto-written summaries; "Build with Otto", LinkedIn and JSON Resume import; Import from GitHub; share links; a **portfolio** (one public page per account at `/p/<slug>`, made from a resume in one of six web templates; Pro and Max, the trial included, can build and publish it; a page whose owner drops to Free goes offline); an applications tracker with follow-up reminders, interview prep and skill gaps; a work profile with a job post eligibility check; invites, feedback and yearly plans; first-run tours; Sentry and PostHog. `../FEATURES.md` lists every feature with its plan and status. There is deliberately **no admin page**: the owner reads users, payments and feedback in the Supabase and Dodo Payments dashboards.

Audience: job seekers worldwide. Use English, USD, and global examples (not India-specific). Tone: plain everyday words, short sentences, warm and a little playful, never cheesy. Otto speaks in short lines.

## User flow

```
Landing (/) → Studio (/studio)
  1 Upload    drop PDF/DOCX · or "Start from scratch" · or "Try a sample"
  2 Review    recruiter view: checks list + "what hiring software reads" text (only for uploads)
  3 Fix       edit every section; lint badges on each line; "Fix with Otto" (AI, sign-in)
  4 Tailor    paste a job link (job text as backup) → keywords have/missing, questions, reorder, summary → tailored copy (AI, sign-in)
  5 Download  preview + free PDF download + "check my new PDF" (before vs after)
Sign-in: "Continue with Google", or email → 6-digit code (dialog inside the studio, or /login). Account: /account.

Dashboard (/dashboard, signed in): Home · Jobs · Resumes (+ shared links) · Applications (Board / List, + /[id] hub, /[id]/prep) · Formats · Portfolio · Recently deleted · Account
Public: /s/[slug] (a shared resume, server-rendered, PDF button; its owner, checked against the live session, also sees a bar with the views, Edit and link settings) · /p/[slug] (a portfolio, cached, revalidated on change)
```

AI actions need Pro, Max or the Pro trial (to control cost); Free and signed-out calls get 402 `upgrade_required`, which every AI button turns into one trial prompt (`UpgradeDialog`), not an error. Import (rule-based reader), the recruiter view, editing, lint and PDF download need no account.

Pro tools follow the same pattern. Server: `src/app/api/pro-tools.ts` (`requireProTool`, `proToolRequired`) answers 402 `upgrade_required` on `POST/PATCH /api/applications*`, `POST /api/share`, `PUT /api/profile`, `PUT /api/jobs/saved/[id]`, and on `PUT /api/resumes/[id]` for a new resume past `resumeLimit(plan)` (`saveBackup`'s `plan_limit`); `POST /api/jobs/matches` answers Free with `locked: true`, the counts and no jobs; the follow-up cron skips Free accounts; `findPublicShare` downloads a Free owner's Pro format in Classic. Reads, deletes, edits of existing resumes and turning share links off stay open on every plan, so an account past a limit keeps everything it made. Client: `src/components/account/pro-tools.ts` (`useProTools()`, `unlockProTool(tool)`, `roomForResume()`) checks first and opens the prompt through `openUpgradePrompt(tool)` (`upgrade-prompt.tsx`; `<UpgradePromptHost />` in the app shell, the studio shell and `/account`). `forPlan(resume, plan)` in `src/lib/pdf/templates.ts` is the download copy (a Pro format on Free comes out in Classic). Cloud sync treats `upgrade_required` (and the 50-resume cap's 429 `limit_reached`) as no room, so a resume made before signing in stays in that browser; it is offered again only after the next read of the account, a deletion that frees a slot, or a plan change, not on every edit.

## Stack (installed, do not add packages)

Next.js 16.4.5 (App Router, `src/`, Turbopack), React 19.3, TypeScript 6.0.3 (strict, `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`), Tailwind CSS 4.3 (`@theme` tokens in `src/app/globals.css`, no tailwind.config), zod 4.6 (`z.email()`, not `z.string().email()`), zustand 5.1, @tanstack/react-query 5.104 (the signed-in app's server data, added 2026-11-11 with the owner's approval; see Code conventions), drizzle-orm 0.45 (pg-core) + postgres 3.4.9 (postgres.js, Supabase Postgres in production) + @electric-sql/pglite 0.5.15 (dev dependency: the local `.pglite/` database in `bun dev` and the in-memory database in tests), @supabase/supabase-js 2.119 + @supabase/ssr 0.12.7 (Supabase Auth: email codes, Google, cookie sessions), unpdf 2.1 (pdf.js text and positions), mammoth 1.13 + jszip 3.10 (DOCX), @react-pdf/renderer 4.9 (client-side PDF export), OpenRouter Chat Completions over `fetch` (strict JSON-schema structured output, `provider.require_parameters` + `data_collection: "deny"`; no SDK), Dodo Payments over `fetch` (no SDK, which retries POSTs although Dodo has no idempotency keys: `src/lib/billing/dodo.ts` for the API, our own Standard Webhooks check with `node:crypto` for webhooks, and Dodo's hosted checkout page, so no payment script loads on our site), lucide-react 1.70 (icons), clsx + tailwind-merge (`cn()` in `src/lib/utils.ts`), @sentry/nextjs 11.11 (error reports), posthog-node 5.63 (product events; no posthog-js: the browser sends a first-party beacon), web-vitals 6.2 (page speed, loaded for a sample of page loads), resend 6.37 (the trial-ending email and one-time service notices), no tour library (first-run tips are our own non-modal popover in `src/components/onboarding/run-tour.tsx`), vitest 5 (node environment), pdf-lib (tests only: building fixture PDFs). Package manager: **bun** (`bun.lock`). Node 24.

If a library API is unclear, read its type definitions in `node_modules/<pkg>/` (`.d.ts`) instead of guessing.

Browser builds swap three parts of the PDF renderer that never run there for `src/lib/pdf/shims/` (`turbopack.resolveAlias` in `next.config.ts`, about 116 KB gzip): `@react-pdf/reconciler`'s builds for React 18 and 19.0, fontkit's Brotli decoder (WOFF2 only) and `@react-pdf/hyphenate`'s English hyphenator. So keep React at 19.2 or later, PDF fonts TTF or OTF, and `keepWordsWhole` registered as the hyphenation callback (`src/components/pdf/fonts.ts`); `src/lib/pdf/shims/shims.test.ts` checks all three. Vitest loads the Node build, so a change to these aliases needs `next build` and a PDF preview in the browser.

## Demo modes (the app must fully work locally with zero keys)

| Missing env | Behavior |
|---|---|
| `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` | Import uses `heuristicParse()`. Fix and tailor return deterministic demo results. The UI shows a small "Demo mode" note. |
| Supabase (any env) | Development: dev sign-in (`src/lib/auth/dev.ts`): request-code returns the code as `devCode` and the form shows it; verify sets a signed, revocable `otto_dev_session` cookie; Google is disabled. `DEV_AUTH=supabase` switches development to real Supabase. Production: request-code and Google answer 503 `auth_unavailable` ("Sign-in isn't set up yet"); everything signed-out still works. Dev sign-in can't link GitHub: `DEV_GITHUB_LOGIN` makes every dev account connected to that login. |
| `DATABASE_URL` / `POSTGRES_URL` | Development: the local `.pglite/` folder (PGlite, migrated on first use), which is also used whenever dev sign-in is on, so test accounts never reach a real database. Production: `getDb()` throws. Tests: an in-memory PGlite per file (`vitest.setup.ts`). |
| Dodo values | Paid mode (`"dodo"`) needs `DODO_API_KEY`, `DODO_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `BILLING_SIGNING_SECRET` and `DODO_PRO_MONTHLY_PRODUCT_ID`, and a key whose prefix (`dodo_test_` / `dodo_live_`) matches `DODO_ENVIRONMENT` (a mismatch logs `billing.key_environment_mismatch` once, since every call would get a 401). Without them `billingMode()` is `"mock"` in development (upgrade flips the plan locally, Pro or Max; a new account's Pro monthly checkout starts a local 3-day trial) and `"disabled"` in production ("Payments aren't live yet, email us"). Pro yearly, each Max option and the trial show only while their product id is set (`DODO_PRO_YEARLY_PRODUCT_ID`, `DODO_MAX_MONTHLY_PRODUCT_ID` / `_YEARLY_`, `DODO_PRO_TRIAL_PRODUCT_ID`). `DODO_ENVIRONMENT` picks test.dodopayments.com unless it is `live` (or `live_mode`, `production`), so a missing or unknown value never takes real money. |
| `GITHUB_TOKEN` | Import from GitHub still works, at GitHub's shared 60 calls an hour for the server. |
| `CRON_SECRET` | `/api/cron/*` answer 401, so no cleanup, follow-up reminders or trial-ending reminders run. Unsubscribe links can't be signed. |
| `RESEND_API_KEY` / `EMAIL_FROM` | Follow-up and trial-ending reminders stay in the dashboard bell; the Account page's reminder card shows no email switch. |
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DSN` | Sentry isn't loaded or wrapped into the build; nothing is reported. Dev reports only with `NEXT_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DEV=1`. |
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_POSTHOG_KEY` | No beacon is sent and nothing reaches PostHog (also off in tests and under Do Not Track / GPC). |

## Scheduled jobs

`vercel.json` runs `/api/cron/cleanup` at 04:17 UTC, `/api/cron/jobs` at 05:40 UTC and `/api/cron/follow-ups` at 14:00 UTC (production only). Cleanup and follow-ups stop after about 45 s; the jobs ingest has `maxDuration` 300 and stops starting boards after 240 s. All resume the next day; running one twice is harmless. Cleanup deletes by age in batches of 1,000 (the retention table in [database.md](database.md)). Follow-ups: applications still "applied" whose `follow_up_at` has come and isn't reminded yet, on accounts that aren't on Free (the tracker is Pro; a Free account's due reminders wait, unclaimed), get bell notifications only; no email goes out. Emails sent before had signed Done, Snooze 3 days and Unsubscribe links; each opens `/reminder`, which acts only when its one button is pressed, so link scanners that run pages change nothing (mail apps' own unsubscribe still posts `List-Unsubscribe` one-click straight to `/api/notifications/unsubscribe`). Jobs: syncs `job_boards` with the seed list, reads the feeds once a day (Remote OK, the newest 200 Himalayas jobs, Jobicy's last 7 days down to what the last read saw) beside the company boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee, Personio), which go least recently read first, 4 at a time, each worker taking the oldest board whose host may be asked now (each host at its own pace: Greenhouse 0.3 s, Ashby 0.5 s, Lever, Recruitee, Personio and Himalayas 1 s, Jobicy 2 s, Workable 3 s), skipping any read in the last 20 hours. Per board it keeps the newest 1,000, normalizes only new or changed jobs (content hash) and writes an unchanged job only when it was missing or closed, reads skills, level, years and the work-rights lines from the whole post and then drops the text (only Himalayas and Remote OK, which can't be read again, keep 6,000 characters; `/jobs/[id]` and the Tailor flow read the post live, `full-post.ts`, cached a day), leaves lines most of the board's jobs share out of the skills, dedupes by (source, external id) and by company + title + location (the employer's own board beats a feed's copy), and closes jobs missing from 2 reads in a row; 2 404s in a row close the whole board. Then it closes jobs past their end date and feed jobs older than 30 days, deletes rows closed 7+ days (30+ when saved or linked to an application), and trims to 80,000 rows. `bun scripts/jobs-ingest.ts [--limit N]` runs the same code against the local `.pglite/`.

Trial reminders (`/api/cron/trial-reminders`, 13:30 UTC, `src/lib/cron/trial-reminders.ts`): Dodo's own Subscription Trial Ending email is turned off in its dashboard (for a trial under 7 days it goes out the moment the trial starts), so accounts on a paid plan with `plan_status` `trialing` (or `mock_trialing`) whose `trial_ends_at` falls up to 36 hours after the start of the run's hour (the day before's run reached 12 hours past it, so normally 12 to 36 hours' notice; a trial a failed or cut-short run missed is still reminded while at least an hour is left) get one bell notification and one Resend email (to every trialist, not only those with reminder emails on; bell only without `RESEND_API_KEY`/`EMAIL_FROM`) giving the end date and time (the work profile's time zone, else UTC), the price after it and a link to `/account` to cancel (the bell links to `/dashboard/account`); a trial set to cancel (`scheduled_cancel`) gets none. Each is recorded in `trial_reminders` (`user_id`, `trial_ends_at`) before the email goes out, and an end date within 3 days of one already reminded counts as the same trial, so a moved end date isn't reminded twice.

Interview reminders and follow-up rows (`/api/cron/follow-ups`, `src/lib/cron/follow-ups.ts`): the follow-up run also reminds the day before an interview: an application in Interviewing whose `interview_at` falls after now and up to 36 hours after the start of the run's hour (the board saves the day as local noon, so the day before's run sees it in every zone), once per interview day (`interview_reminded_for`). Each gets a bell row ("Your interview with Stripe is tomorrow", in the work profile's time zone, else UTC) linking to `/dashboard/applications/<id>/prep?start=1`. Up to 3 follow-ups due in one run get a bell row each, linking to the application, with Write it for me (`?write=follow-up` opens the outreach writer as a follow-up), Done and Snooze on the newest row while it is still due; more share one row. The run makes no AI calls.

## Design direction

`design.md` is the design language and overrides anything here: paper, ink and Otto's orange pencil. In short: depth from surface shade (cream paper, stone panels, white cards on stone) instead of borders or shadows; ink for text, every main button (black pills) and the focus ring; the orange accent only as a pencil mark, at most two per screen; EB Garamond for h1/h2 at regular weight, Inter for everything else. Status colors stay pass = green, warn = amber, fail = red, always with an icon and a word. Mobile-first: everything must work at 360px wide. Light theme only for v1.

Otto is an otter with small reading glasses, drawn in thin sepia lines with flat fills. Poses: `wave`, `read`, `think`, `point`, `cheer`, `worried`, `peek`. He speaks through `OttoNote` (a small line-drawn head, a gray meta line, a first-person message) in the studio and in dialogs; the full body is for empty states, success moments, the 404, the final call to action and the share images. Blinking is his only idle motion. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Code conventions

- Comments: production style, explaining *why*, never *what*. No commented-out code. No banner comments.
- Tailwind 4: built-in utilities over arbitrary values (`size-8`, not `h-[32px] w-[32px]`; `gap-3`, not `gap-[12px]`). Arbitrary values only when no utility exists. Use theme tokens from `globals.css` (e.g. `bg-ink`, `text-muted`), not raw hex colors in components.
- Components: one component per file when it's over ~100 lines; files under ~300 lines; colocate page-only sections in `_components/`.
- Client components start with `"use client"`. Never import server-only modules (`src/lib/env.ts`, `src/db/**`, `src/lib/auth/session.ts`, `codes.ts`, `src/lib/usage.ts`, `src/lib/rate-limit.ts`, `src/lib/billing/index.ts`, `src/lib/ai/**` except `types.ts`, `src/lib/parse/**` except `types.ts`, `src/lib/http.ts`, `src/lib/github/{api,cache,draft,http}.ts`, `src/lib/cron/**`, `src/lib/notifications/{records,email,follow-up,unsubscribe}.ts`, every `records.ts`, `src/lib/job-feed/{parsers,normalize,store,ingest,rank}.ts`, `src/lib/portfolio/{records,cache,page,sync,content-records,screen}.ts`, `src/lib/github/profile.ts`, `src/lib/analytics/server.ts`) from client code. Types-only files are client-safe.
- zustand selectors that build arrays or objects must use `useShallow` (or the provided hooks), or React 19 loops.
- Server data in the signed-in app goes through TanStack Query: one client per tab (`src/lib/queries/client.ts`, `getQueryClient()`; hooks pass it with `useAppQueryClient()`, so no provider is needed and a component works on any page), keys in `keys.ts` (resource, then account id; the cache is emptied when the account signs out or changes), fetchers and fresh-for times in `options.ts` (5 minutes; applications also refetch when the tab comes back after that, since every change made in the tab patches the cache or drops it), one small hook per resource next to the screen that uses it. The dashboard's main hooks (applications, work profile, writings, prep packs, shares, notifications, setup) take the account id from `useSessionUserId()` (`components/account/session-user.tsx`): inside the dashboard it is the id the layout verified, so their requests start at hydration; elsewhere it is `useMe()`'s. Quick actions change the cache at once and put it back if the server says no (`use-applications.ts`: a new application gets its id in the browser, `POST /api/applications` keeps it). Sidebar and phone-tab links prefetch the page and its main data once a hover rests 120 ms, or at once on focus, touch or press (`components/dashboard/prefetch.ts`, `hover-intent.ts`); the Jobs prefetch sends `x-jobs-prefetch`, so its page is logged as seen (`POST /api/jobs/events`, one batch, 20 batches a minute per account) only when the Jobs tab shows it. Not on it yet: `/api/me` (marketing pages read it too, and React Query would add ~10 KB there), notifications, the first-login setup, the application hub and prep pages, the portfolio content editor.
- Client code never imports zod: request schemas live in a `validation.ts` beside each feature's client-safe types (`lib/resume`, `applications`, `writings`, `job-feed`, `eligibility`, `setup`, `notifications`, `share`, `feedback`, `portfolio`), which only route handlers, server code and tests import (the portfolio content schemas: `lib/portfolio/content-validation.ts`). Plain limits the browser needs sit in `config/plans.ts` (`DRAFT_LIMITS`, `SUMMARY_IMPROVE_CHARS`) or a zod-free module (`lib/insights/prep-rounds.ts`), never in `lib/api/types.ts`. No page loads zod up front, only lazy chunks such as the studio editor (`components/studio/editor/limits.ts`), the resume file import and the GitHub import dialog. `src/client-imports.test.ts` follows the static imports of the studio, portfolio editor, interview prep and root error pages, the setup checklist and the pages with click-only dialogs, and fails when zod, the lint rules, the PDF font tables, the mascot copy table or one of those dialogs comes back.
- Dialogs most visits never open load their code when the browser is idle or on first open (`components/ui/lazy.tsx`, `Lazy`), not with the page; React.lazy is avoided for the reason in `components/studio/step-loader.ts`. The wrappers are `components/account/lazy-dialogs.tsx` (upgrade, sign-in), one module per dashboard dialog (`dashboard/letters/lazy-write-dialog.tsx`, `applications/lazy-application-dialog.tsx`, `interview/lazy-prepare-dialog.tsx`, `jobs/lazy-outreach-dialog.tsx`, so a page's bundle counts only the dialogs it can open) and `components/feedback/lazy-feedback-dialog.tsx`; render a wrapper before its first opening too (closed), since that is what starts the idle load (free accounts skip the write dialog, which they can't open), and put the per-opening `key` on the wrapper.
- React 19 lint rules are on (react-hooks v7): no synchronous `setState` inside `useEffect` bodies, no reading refs during render. Derive state instead of syncing it.
- Accessibility: real `<button>`/`<label>`, visible focus rings, `aria-live` for async results, dialogs trap focus and close on Escape, 44px minimum touch targets.
- Menus render their items only while open (`components/ui/menu.tsx`), so a ⋯ menu on every card costs one button until it's used; code or tests that look for an item open the menu first.
- Errors shown to users are plain and actionable ("That file isn't a PDF or Word document."). No raw error codes in the UI.

## Production and scale

Target: deploy on Vercel (Fluid compute, functions in `iad1`) with Supabase (Postgres + Auth, US East), and scale horizontally with no code changes from 10 users to 100k. Owner steps: `setup.md`.

- **Database access.** Only our server talks to Postgres, through Supabase's transaction pooler (`DATABASE_URL`, port 6543, postgres.js with `prepare: false`, 5 connections per instance). Every table has RLS on and no policies, so the Data API (publishable key) sees nothing. Schema changes are new files in `supabase/migrations/`; the production build applies them (`bun scripts/migrate.ts --deploy`) after `next build` succeeds and before the new code goes live, so the old code briefly runs on the new schema: drop, tighten or rewrite only what the live code no longer needs (handoff.md section 5). Never edit an applied file.

- **Stateless servers.** Any instance can serve any request. No per-user state in module memory; only lazy singletons (DB client, SDK clients) and pure constants. Everything durable lives in the DB, everything per-user in the session cookie or the browser.
- **Bounded everything.** Every input has a size cap (`TEXT_LIMITS`, `UPLOAD_LIMITS`, zod `.max()`), every list a length cap, every external call a timeout. Uploads stay under Vercel's 4.5 MB request body limit (`UPLOAD_LIMITS.maxBytes` = 4 MB).
- **Atomic, idempotent writes.** Counters use single-statement upserts (no read-then-write races). Capped inserts (50 resume backups, applications) and backup deletes run in a transaction that first locks the user's row (`lockUser`), because Postgres, unlike SQLite, runs writers side by side. Webhooks insert their id and apply the change in one transaction, so a redelivery is a no-op, and an event older than the last one applied for the same subscription (`users.billing_event_at`) changes nothing. PUTs are upserts keyed by `(user_id, id)`, written only over a copy that isn't newer and, with a base, only over the version the edit was made on; sending the version the account already holds again is answered as saved and writes nothing. Foreign keys with `on delete cascade` from every per-user table to `users`, and from `users` to `auth.users`, mean nothing is ever orphaned.
- **Billing statuses (Dodo).** The plan comes from the subscription in each webhook, never from the event name (Dodo sends the subscription's latest state, in any order), and `plan_status` stores our word for it, mapped by `internalStatus()` in `dodo-status.ts`. Dodo has no trialing status and no trial-end field, so the trial is derived: `active` inside the trial window (`trial_period_days` above 0 and a billing period, from `previous_billing_date` or else `created_at` to `next_billing_date`, no longer than the trial plus an hour and starting no later than that after `created_at`, so a short later period after a `keep_cycle` plan change or an `on_hold` recovery is never a trial) → `trialing`, the product's plan with `trial_ends_at` = `next_billing_date` (trial allowance); `active` with `cancel_at_next_billing_date` → `scheduled_cancel` (`plan_renews_at` null; a trial keeps its end date); any other `active`, `past_due` (a failed renewal during the 3-day grace period, while Dodo retries) and `scheduled_cancel` → the plan (`active` ends a trial; a failed first full charge leaves the dates on the trial window, so it keeps the trial's allowance and still counts as the one trial); `on_hold` (the grace period ran out) → Free, but still live and chargeable, so account deletion cancels it and a new purchase goes to the portal to fix the card; `paused`, and `cancelled` or `expired` (stored as `canceled`) → Free, from the tracked subscription only. `pending` and `failed` (a checkout whose first payment failed) change nothing and are logged at info; any other status changes nothing and is logged as a warning. `plan_renews_at` is `next_billing_date` and `plan_period_*` runs from `previous_billing_date` (else `created_at`) to it. A refused second trial is cancelled and its trial payment refunded (`POST /refunds` by `payment_id`, claimed per payment, so it happens once whichever of `subscription.*` or `payment.succeeded` comes first; a failed refund frees the claim and logs `billing.reused_trial_refund_failed`). `billing.trial_not_detected` warns when a trial-product subscription isn't seen as a trial in its first 3 days, `billing.trial_upgrade_requested` and `billing.plan_change_requested` log plan changes, and `billing.plan_change_payment_failed` logs a declined plan-change charge. Dropping to Free takes a published portfolio offline (kept) and drops its cached page. For a Dodo customer, `/api/me` sends `billing.paymentIssue` (`on_hold`) and `billing.cancelScheduled` (`scheduled_cancel`): on hold, the account page shows **Manage billing** with why and no upgrade offer; set to end, the upgrade card and dialog ask to resume it in **Manage billing** instead of offering a charge.
- **Allowance periods.** `usage` rows are keyed by period. A trial counts in one fixed row, `"trial"`, so neither a month turning nor a moved end date opens a second allowance; it ends with the first full-price payment. Free counts per UTC calendar month (`"2026-11"`, resets on the 1st). Monthly and yearly plans count per billing month (`"cycle:<ISO start>"`): a monthly plan from one renewal to the next (the stored billing period, rolling on a month at a time if a renewal webhook is late), a yearly plan in monthly windows from the day it started (in a shorter month, on its last day); with no stored period it falls back to the calendar month. Moving between Pro and Max or between monthly and yearly between renewals carries what was used over (`carryUsageOver`, in the same transaction as the plan change), so a switch never starts an allowance over; a renewal does, and so does the first payment after a trial. Rows with older `week:` keys, from the retired weekly plans, are no longer read. Invite bonuses land on the account's current period row. `/api/me` returns `period: { kind, resetsAt }` so meters and notes show the reset date; a 429 `limit_reached` has `Retry-After` until the reset.
- **AI budget.** An abuse guard on top of the feature limits: tokens per allowance period (`AI_TOKEN_BUDGETS`, `aiTokenBudget()`: Pro 5M and Max 10M a month, the trial 500K in total, none on Free). `runStructured` hands every response's billed `usage` to an AsyncLocalStorage meter (`src/lib/ai/token-meter.ts`); AI routes run their AI work inside `spendAiTokens` (`src/lib/ai-budget.ts`, reached through `runMetered`, `takeAccountUnit().spend` and `claimAiRead().spend`), which adds the total to the `usage` row with feature `ai_tokens` on the same period key, failed but billed calls included (demo mode adds nothing). The write runs in `after()`, once the answer is out (the budget is soft). `checkAiBudget` runs before a unit is taken: at or over the budget the call gets 429 `limit_reached` with `reason: "ai_budget"` and no unit is taken (uploads go to the basic reader). `/api/me` returns `aiBudget: { used, limit }`.
- **Safety checks.** `checkContent()` (`src/lib/ai/moderation.ts`) sends text to `meta-llama/llama-guard-4-12b` on OpenRouter (`data_collection: "deny"`, temperature 0, 6 s timeout, 8,000-character chunks, 4 at a time) and blocks only S3, S4 and S12 (sexual content); other categories are logged as `moderation.flagged` (ids only, never the text). It fails open (`moderation.unavailable`) on a timeout, provider error or unreadable answer, and is skipped without an API key. `runStructured` checks every answer except imports (`structure`) before returning it; a block is an `AIError("refused")`, so metered routes answer 422 `ai_refused` with their own copy and the capped refund, and Build with Otto / GitHub drafts fall back to their non-AI result. Public pages: `POST /api/share` checks the shared copy; `POST/PATCH /api/portfolio` and `PUT /api/portfolio/content` go through `screenedWrite()` (content saves through `screenedWriteWith()`, reading only `published` and `updatedAt`), which checks what would go live (the whole page when publishing; only new resume copy, address or content on a live page) and writes only if the row's `updatedAt` hasn't moved since (else re-checks, up to 3 tries, then 409); a resume save syncing into a live page is checked after the save has answered, the word pre-check reading only lines the live copy wasn't checked with (it passed when it went live; email, phone and link labels never count as checked; a hit still sends the whole page), and skipped if blocked. Blocked pages answer 422 `content_blocked` and stay as they were. Email and phone are never sent; voice typing has no separate check.
- **Indexed access only.** Every query hits a primary key or an index; no full scans in request paths; per-user queries are always filtered by `user_id`.
- **Push work to the edge or client.** Marketing pages are static (CDN). PDF rendering and lint run in the browser. Only parsing, auth and AI touch the server.
- **Fail soft.** AI failures fall back (heuristic parse, a clear retry message), refund usage, and never lose user data. Background sync failures retry quietly.
- **Swap points for real scale** (don't build now): `rate-limit.ts` / `usage.ts` can move to Redis (Upstash) behind the same signatures; AI calls can move to a queue if latency ever matters.
- **Observability.** Structured logs via `src/lib/log.ts` with stable event names (`import.parse_failed`, `ai.refused`, `billing.webhook_invalid`…); every AI answer logs `ai.usage` (counts only: task, model, provider, attempt, time, tokens). No PII in logs (mask emails, never log resume text, codes or tokens). Errors also go to Sentry (`src/lib/observability/`: errors, plus server traces for `SENTRY_TRACES_SAMPLE_RATE` of requests (5% by default) and none in the browser; scrubbed of emails, phones, IPs, bodies, cookies and `/s/<token>` paths; SDK 11's `dataCollection` set to collect no user info, cookies, bodies, query strings or local variables; browser reports go through our own `/api/relay` tunnel, which forwards only to our project, 30 a minute per address; failed cron steps are reported too; `log.error` fields with a `userId` name the account by that id). Server reports are flushed inside `after()`, so a function frozen after its response doesn't drop them. In the browser the SDK loads once the page is idle on the app and sign-in pages (`isAppPage`), and elsewhere only when something breaks; uncaught errors from before it loads are held and sent once it's up. Product events go to PostHog with no PostHog script in the browser (`src/lib/analytics/`): the root layout's `<Analytics />` queues page views (the route template from `usePathname` + `useParams`, so `/p/[slug]`, never the slug), named events and, for 20% of page loads, web vitals, and sends them as one JSON `fetch` (keepalive) to `/api/activity` once idle or as the page hides; the route checks the session claim, links the local-storage visitor id to the account once (`$identify`), records app-page activity as before, and forwards everything to PostHog's `/batch/` in one request inside `after()`. Anonymous visitors get no person profile; no autocapture, no recordings, no cookies; skipped under Do Not Track / GPC, in the browser and on the server. Both are off without their keys.
- **Security.** Security headers in `next.config.ts`; httpOnly + sameSite=lax + secure cookies (Supabase's `sb-...` session cookies are made httpOnly too: no browser code ever needs the tokens, so there is no browser Supabase client; the one script-readable cookie, `otto_si=1` from `components/account/session-hint.ts`, only hints that this browser is signed in and holds nothing secret); sign-in codes generated, emailed and checked by Supabase, with our per-address and per-IP limits on top; the Supabase secret key is server-only and used only to delete auth users; constant-time compares; webhook signatures verified; resume text inside AI prompts is treated as data (prompt-injection safe). JSON routes require `application/json` (`rejectNonJson`); routes that can't (uploads, logout) refuse cross-site requests (`rejectCrossSite`: `Sec-Fetch-Site`, else `Origin`).
- **Revocable sessions.** `getCurrentUser()` asks Supabase on every call (`auth.getUser()`, not the local-only `getClaims()`), so signing out (scope `local`: this browser only) or deleting the account stops a copied cookie at once. Within one page render React `cache()` shares that answer; route handlers have no render, so each call asks again (pass the user along instead). The `users` row is read while `getUser()` runs, keyed by the cookie's unchecked user id, and used only when `getUser()` confirms that id. A Supabase outage makes it throw instead of returning null, so nobody gets signed out by it. `src/proxy.ts` refreshes the session on every page request (`updateSession`, `getClaims()`), and only its "/" → /dashboard hint and the share page's owner check (`currentUserIdHint()`, which only decides whether a view counts) trust the JWT alone (signature checked, revocation not). It skips public portfolios (`/p/*`, which never read the session, so a view or its preview image costs no proxy call) and API routes: each one that reads the session asks Supabase itself through `getCurrentUser()`, whose `getUser()` refreshes an expired token and writes the cookies from the route, so an API call costs one Supabase lookup, not two. Once the project signs its JWTs with an asymmetric key (setup.md), the proxy's `getClaims()` verifies pages locally with no Supabase call at all. All Auth calls of one Supabase client (one per lookup) share a single 8 s deadline (`fetchWithDeadline`, `src/lib/supabase/deadline.ts`, in the server and proxy clients), so an Auth stall fails the request with a 500 instead of hanging to `maxDuration`; a failing token refresh still sits through auth-js's retry backoff (20-30 s). The dev sign-in cookie is HMAC-signed and must match a `dev_auth.sessions` row; it only exists in development.
- **Deletes sync across devices.** Deleting a backed-up resume writes a tombstone in `user_settings.deleted_resumes`. `GET/POST /api/resumes` return `deletedIds` so other devices drop their copies, and a PUT for a tombstoned id returns `{ deleted: true }` instead of bringing it back.
- **Edits sync without silent loss.** Each PUT names the account's version the edit was made on (`?base=`); an edit is always dated after the copy it changes, even on a device whose clock runs behind. When the account moved on, the PUT writes nothing and answers `{ stale: true }`; the device reads the account again and, where both copies changed since they last matched (the `synced` versions in the `otto-sync-v1` ledger), keeps the newer one and saves the other as an auto version ("Edits on this device" or "Edits from another device") with a toast. Sign-out releases a resume only when the account holds this copy, or a newer one and this copy has no edits of its own.
- **The studio saves in the background, safely across tabs.** `otto-studio-v1` is written 0.4 s after the last change (at most 2 s later), at once when the tab hides or closes, and right after sign-out releases the account's resumes; an edit that changes nothing writes nothing. Before writing, and whenever another tab saves or this one comes back into view, a tab folds the stored studio into its own record by record (`tab-merge.ts`): the newer copy of each resume wins, saved versions, trash and checks merge, and removals travel with the save as dated tombstones (`removed`, 60 days), so an old tab never undoes a newer one and nothing missing is taken as deleted; the open resume and step stay per tab. Another tab's changes are never uploaded or deleted from the account by this tab. When storage is full, saves leave out the trash first, then saved versions. Cloud sync uploads 5 s after the last edit (at most 30 s later), sends unsaved edits with `keepalive` (within 60 KB) when the page hides or closes, re-reads the account when a tab comes back after 30 s, and hides the "Loading your resumes" screen as soon as the account has been read, with uploads carrying on behind it. `/studio` asks for the list alongside `/api/me` when the browser has backed up one account (and only one) before.
- **Account deletion cancels billing first.** `DELETE /api/account` cancels every live Dodo subscription immediately (`PATCH /subscriptions/{id}` with `status: cancelled`), trials, `on_hold` and `pending` ones included (the stored one plus any on our products under the stored customer or a customer with the same email, found with `GET /customers?email=` and kept only on an exact match). The lookup runs for every account, even one with no ids stored, since a paid checkout stores nothing until its webhook is applied; a failed lookup is logged (`billing.lookup_failed`) and doesn't block the deletion. If a cancel fails, the account is kept and the route returns 502. A refused cancel is read back: a subscription already ended, or already set to end, counts as done; Dodo answering 404 counts as a failure (`billing.cancel_not_found`, since test and live ids look alike). In production without `DODO_API_KEY`, an account with a subscription that could still charge is kept and the route returns 503. Stored ids that aren't in Dodo's format (old Paddle or Creem ids) count as nothing at Dodo and are never sent there; the same-email lookup still runs for such an account. Then our rows go in one transaction, then the Supabase Auth user (`auth.admin.deleteUser`, ending every session). If that last step fails the route answers 500 and the still-valid session lets a retry finish. The webhook is the backstop, in production only (a laptop may hold other accounts, which is also why `.env.local` gets its own `BILLING_SIGNING_SECRET`): a live subscription event whose signed `user_id` has no `users` row (accounts exist before any checkout, so that account was deleted) is checked with Dodo (`GET /subscriptions/{id}`); if it can still charge, it is cancelled at once, each succeeded charge is refunded once (the refused-trial refund and its claims), and `billing.orphan_subscription_cancelled` is logged as an error. An `orphan:<subscription id>` event row in `billing_log` (not a `replacement` row, which goes with the account) marks it so a later `payment.succeeded` is refunded too. A failed lookup or cancel answers 500, so Dodo's redelivery is the retry. One Dodo says has already ended or is set to end, as after the deletion's own cancel, keeps its charges; an unsigned id, or a `pending` or ended status, is only logged. Checkout sends anyone with a live subscription to a plan change or the portal instead of starting a second one, and Dodo's Allow Multiple Subscriptions setting is off as a second lock.
- **Sign-in.** Email: `requestLoginCode` (our limits, then `signInWithOtp`, `shouldCreateUser: true`) and `verifyLoginCode` (`verifyOtp`, type `email`). Google: `POST /api/auth/google` returns Supabase's authorize URL (PKCE verifier in an httpOnly cookie, `redirectTo` = `appUrl()/auth/callback?next=<safe path>`), and `/auth/callback` exchanges the code. Either way our `users` row (same id as the auth user) is created on the first signed-in request by `ensureUser`.

## Testing

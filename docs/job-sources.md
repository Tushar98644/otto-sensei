# Job sources

Where the Jobs tab and `/jobs` get their openings, what each source's terms allow, and why some were left out. Checked on 2026-11-08; Workable, Recruitee, Personio, Jobicy and SmartRecruiters on 2026-11-10; Workday, Freshteam, Zoho Recruit, Keka, Darwinbox, SAP SuccessFactors and Himalayas search on 2026-11-22, when the owner approved Workday; Gem, Pinpoint, Jobvite and the US-heavy applicant-tracking systems on 2026-11-23. Re-check a source's terms before changing how its jobs are used.

Rules that apply to every source we use:

- Only public, documented APIs that need no login, no key and no bot check. Never LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, or anything behind a login or a challenge page. One exception, the owner's call on 2026-11-22: Workday career sites, whose JSON is public but undocumented (below). USAJOBS needs a free key and is built but off until the owner registers one (below).
- robots.txt is respected (each host's file was read; none disallows the paths we call, and Lever's `Crawl-delay: 1` is honored). Each Workday tenant's file is read again before every read of its site, and a site whose file disallows the job search is skipped.
- Every job names its source ("via Greenhouse") and links to the original posting. That link is a normal followed link (`rel="noopener"`, no `nofollow`).
- Reads go through the app's SSRF-safe transport (`src/lib/jobs/fetch.ts`), say who we are (`OttoResumeBot/1.0`), send no cookies, and keep a per-host pace (`HOST_SPACING_MS` in `src/lib/job-feed/ingest.ts`). Workday's job search is the one JSON POST; the transport refuses to follow a redirect on it.
- Matching is rule-based on our own server. Job text never goes to the AI unless the person presses "Tailor my resume", which is the same as pasting the link themselves.
- JobPosting structured data (`/jobs/[id]`) only where the terms allow republishing, and only with the complete post read live; every other job only links out.
- **No job text is stored for a source whose post can be read again** (every company board, and Jobicy). Ingest reads the whole post once, keeps what matching needs (skills, kind of role, level, years asked, place, the work-rights lines) and drops the text. `/jobs/[id]` and the Tailor step read the post again through the same reader as a pasted link (`src/lib/job-feed/full-post.ts` → `src/lib/jobs/read.ts`), cached a day (a failed read isn't kept, so the next request asks the board again); when that read fails, the page shows the job's facts and the link; Tailor reads the job's link itself, uses the server's read as its backup, and asks for the text only when both fail. Himalayas (its pages refuse bots) and Remote OK (no single-job call) keep up to 6,000 characters, which Tailor uses as the post.

### The seed list

`src/lib/job-feed/seeds.ts`: **761 company boards** (343 Greenhouse, 259 Ashby, 64 Lever, 90 Workable, 3 Recruitee, 2 Personio) and **147 Workday career sites**. Every token was requested and had open jobs: the first 323 on 2026-11-08, mostly tech companies; 282 more on 2026-11-10, all 605 answering that day; 46 more on 2026-11-22, Indian companies and global ones hiring in India, each with live India jobs that day; 67 Workable accounts the same day, each with at least 3 India jobs from the last 90 days; and later that day 43 boards and the 147 Workday sites for the US, the UK, Australia, Latin America and Europe (below).

- **The 147 Workday career sites of 2026-11-22** were found by trying 404 large employers in tech, finance, insurance, healthcare and hospitals, pharma, retail, accounting and consulting across the US, the UK, Australia, Latin America and Europe: each name was tried as a tenant on the common Workday hosts by reading only its robots.txt (an unknown tenant answers 422), which found 186 tenants and lists their career sites. Each site was then asked for one page of jobs with our own parser, and the largest external site per tenant was kept when robots.txt allowed the job search and it had current jobs. On a full local read they hold 36,203 open jobs (35,181 from the last 90 days), and the match index has 21,827 openings in the US, 2,382 in the UK, 744 in Australia, 423 in Brazil, 547 in Mexico and 1,101 in India from them. Left out: tenants with no live site or under about 40 jobs (Autodesk, Qualcomm, Qantas, QBE, Macquarie, Lloyd's of London and others), defense contractors whose roles mostly need clearance or US citizenship (Northrop Grumman, RTX, Booz Allen, Boeing, GE Aerospace), and the many LatAm and EU employers that aren't on Workday (Mercado Libre, Itaú, Ambev, Nestlé, Siemens Healthineers and others). Johnson & Johnson (400) and Thermo Fisher (500) refused the job search that day.
- **The 43 boards of 2026-11-22 for the UK, Australia, Latin America and Europe** were found by trying 326 employer names with many openings there on Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever and Workable (138 answered, 54 of them already seeded), 1 request at a time per service at our usual pace. A board was kept when at least 8 of its jobs from the last 90 days were in those regions by our place rules and its titles and links showed the right company: 3,224 jobs, 2,385 of them from the last 90 days (UK 377, Australia 89, Brazil 381, Mexico 101, all of Latin America 590, the rest of Europe 596). Left out: Jobandtalent (staffing), boards with fewer jobs there (Gymshark, Freetrade, Airtasker, Octopus Deploy and others) and lookalikes (a US "Wise" and "Peak" on Greenhouse).
- **The 282 added on 2026-11-10 were chosen for non-tech roles:** healthcare and biotech 67, finance and insurance 56, retail and consumer 42, manufacturing and energy 20, education 19, logistics and mobility 15, staffing and HR 14, services and sales-led 12, hospitality and travel 11, nonprofit and public 9, media 7, real estate 7, legal 3. By home region: US 191, EU 33, UK 23, Canada 23, Australia 9, other 3 (many hire in several countries).
- **How they were found:** about 1,100 candidate employers in those industries were tried by name on Greenhouse and Ashby (all of them), Lever (about 650 before the run was stopped; Lever answers slowly) and Recruitee and Personio (EU and UK names), and Workable employers were found through Workable's public job search (`jobs.workable.com`, discovery only) and then checked on the documented per-account endpoint. A board was kept when at least 5 open jobs were support, sales, operations, marketing, finance, people, legal, design, product or data roles by `roles.ts`, or 10+ were roles those rules don't name at a non-tech employer (nurses, teachers, store and warehouse staff), and under 60% engineering and data.
- **False hits removed by hand:** a "wise" board that is a field-sales outfit, a "palmetto" that is an animal hospital, "sterling", "remote", "range", "current", "evolve", "lunar", "revel" and others that are different companies; staffing agencies and aggregators on Workable (Huzzle, CXG, Next Job Abroad and the like).
- **Left out on purpose:** boards of mostly on-site manufacturing roles that need US citizenship (SpaceX, Anduril, Rocket Lab, Relativity Space, Lucid Motors; on 2026-11-10 Varda, Archer, Astranis, Divergent, Machina Labs), which would crowd out everything else; boards that are mostly engineering (Speechify, Neuralink, Form Energy).
- **The 67 Workable accounts of 2026-11-22** were found through Workable's public job search filtered to India (`jobs.workable.com/api/v1/jobs?location=India`, 4,057 jobs from 304 employers, discovery only, 2 s between pages). Its results name the employer but not the account, so each employer with 3+ recent India jobs had a few account names tried on the documented per-account endpoint, 3 s apart, keeping one only when its name matched. 48 accounts answered with no jobs (Capgemini, Keywords Studios, Egon Zehnder and others publish through Workable's own job board only) and were skipped. Left out: recruiters, staffing and outsourcing firms and job aggregators (Weekday, Huzzle, Pavago, Fuku, Apt Resources, Crewbloom and similar). Together they had 795 India jobs from the last 90 days by our place rules.
- **Recruitee and Personio** mostly serve small Dutch and German employers posting in their own language; guessing names found few English boards (5 kept). Workable discovery stopped after the 40 largest employers its search returned (1,224 found): more can be added the same way.
- **Visa facts** (`visa-matches.ts`) cover only the first 323 companies.

To add a company: find its board token in its careers page links (`boards.greenhouse.io/<token>`, `jobs.lever.co/<token>`, `jobs.ashbyhq.com/<token>`, `apply.workable.com/<account>`, `<company>.recruitee.com`, `<company>.jobs.personio.de`), check the API answers with jobs, add a line to `seeds.ts`. A board removed from the file has its jobs closed on the next run.

## How many jobs Otto shows

The number on the Jobs tab and `/jobs` ("open jobs") is `countShownJobs` in `records.ts`: rows in `jobs` that are open (`closed_at` null), not a general-application post (`evergreen`, from `isFillerTitle`), and first posted within 90 days (`least(posted_at, first_seen_at)`, so a board's own date counts when it is earlier than our first read). It counts rows, so a job listed in three cities counts three times; matching works on openings (`job_match`, one per `group_key`). The match index stores it as `job_counts.open_jobs` after every ingest.

Measured on 2026-11-22 on a full local read of every board and Workday site (89,624 rows):

| Source | Open | General application | Older than 90 days | Shown | Openings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workday (147 sites) | 36,203 | 63 | 1,004 | 35,136 | 33,654 |
| Greenhouse | 30,225 | 136 | 9,782 | 20,307 | 18,370 |
| Ashby | 13,574 | 38 | 3,509 | 10,027 | 9,509 |
| Workable | 4,189 | 8 | 1,085 | 3,096 | 2,888 |
| Lever | 3,839 | 9 | 2,119 | 1,711 | 1,616 |
| Jobicy, Remote OK, Himalayas | 1,408 | 0 | 0 | 1,408 | 1,380 |
| Recruitee, Personio | 47 | 0 | 7 | 40 | 40 |
| **All** | **89,485** | **254** | **17,506** | **71,725** | **67,300** |

- **The 90-day rule** hides 17,506 open jobs (20%), most of them long-open Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever posts; 180 days would show 80,398 and no age rule 89,231. Dropping general-application posts hides only 254.
- **Openings by country** (match index; a post that names several countries counts in each): US 39,672 (21,827 from Workday), UK 5,388 (2,382), India 3,219 (1,101), Canada 2,676 (655), Germany 2,222 (1,018), Australia 1,441 (744), Brazil 1,114 (423), Mexico 917 (547). 1,181 openings name no place and 45 are open to anywhere.
- **Why production showed 39,976:** the non-Workday boards alone give about 36,600 shown jobs, so production had about 3,400 Workday jobs on 2026-11-22, the day the sites were added. The first read of all 147 sites is about 44,000 post reads at 1.5 s each on at most 2 workers, about 10 days of daily runs. When it finishes the count settles at about **72,000 shown jobs (67,000 openings)**, not 100,000: that is everything the seeded boards list within the current limits.
- **No bug found in the count.** Nothing closed wrongly (the only closes were 136 cross-source duplicates and 3 Workday posts gone), all but 7 boards answered (5 Greenhouse and Ashby tokens are gone and 2 Lever reads failed, all with no jobs), dedupe merges only the same company, title and place (89,231 open rows, 89,231 distinct keys), and the 90-day rule uses the earlier of the board's date and our first read, as documented. Workday posts give an exact start date, so their dates are real, not the list's "Posted 30+ Days Ago".
- **What limits it:** Workday's 300 newest jobs per site (`WORKDAY_LIMITS.perBoard`). The 147 sites list 147,663 jobs; 300 a site keeps at most 38,526 of them. 600 a site would keep 66,739 (about 99,000 shown in all), 1,000 would keep 93,307, but each extra post is a 1.5 s read and the daily runs are already nearly full with Workday, so a higher cap needs more ingest time a day (a second daily run, or more calls per run). That and a longer age window are the owner's call.

## Visa registers

Company facts for the job card ("Listed as a UK visa sponsor") and the work-rights check. Never a promise about one job: a licence or past petitions say a company can and did sponsor, not that it will. Checked on 2026-11-09.

- **Matching is by hand-checked name only** (`src/lib/job-feed/visa-matches.ts`): 198 of the 323 seed companies (113 UK, 172 US names). Candidates were the exact folded name or the name plus a generic word ("Stripe Payments UK Ltd"); each was kept only when its name and town fit the company. Rejected on purpose: "Chime Software Limited" (not Chime), "Kraken Technologies Limited" (Octopus Energy's Kraken, not the exchange), "Sentry Ltd" in Ipswich, "Linear Labs" in Texas, and other lookalikes. Checked by the building agent, not yet by a person. Facts attach by company name (`match-index.ts`), so a feed job from one of those companies (Jobicy lists GitLab, for one) gets the same line.
- **Refresh:** once a month inside the daily jobs cron (when the last check is 30+ days old), or `bun scripts/visa-registers.ts`. A UK file with under 10,000 rows is treated as a broken download and changes nothing.
- **Work-rights check:** a job in the UK or US that would need a visa the person said they need moves from "Likely blocked" to "check" with a note naming the register, unless the post itself says it doesn't sponsor.

## Limits we keep

| What | Where | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Jobs kept per company board (newest first) | `STORE_LIMITS.perBoard` | 1,000 |
| Jobs kept per Workday career site | `WORKDAY_LIMITS.perBoard` | 300, newest first, those in the US, the UK, Australia, Latin America and Europe before the rest, so one giant site can't crowd out the others |
| Workday posts read per site per read | `WORKDAY_LIMITS.postsPerRead`, `readMs` | 120, within 3 minutes and not within 15 s of the call's end; the rest wait, and the site is due again at once, so the next call carries on. No Workday read starts in a call's last minute |
| Rows in `jobs`, open and closed | `STORE_LIMITS.maxRows` | 150,000, a ceiling over the ~100,000 expected with Workday (89,624 rows on a full local read of every board and site on 2026-11-22, plus about a week of closed ones). Measured that day after `VACUUM FULL`: 146 MB for `jobs` and 16 MB for `job_match` at 89,624 and 67,325 rows, so about 18 MB per 10,000 rows with their match rows (a Workday row is about 10% bigger: longer links and ids). 150,000 rows is about 270 MB; with about 50 MB for the rest of the database that leaves about 180 MB of Supabase Free's 500 MB for dead rows between vacuums. Before Workday: 80,000, a ceiling over the ~66,000 expected (about 44,000 open board jobs, 13,000 feed jobs, 9,000 closed in their last week). A row is 1.65 KB in `jobs` plus ~0.2 KB in `job_match` with no stored text (5.5 KB before; measured after `VACUUM FULL`, see MATCHING-RESEARCH.md "More sources") |
| Stored text per job | `SOURCE_INFO[source].liveText`, `JOB_LIMITS.feedDescriptionChars` | None for company boards and Jobicy (read live, cached a day); 6,000 characters for Himalayas and Remote OK |
| Closed after missing from | `STORE_LIMITS.missedRunsToClose` | 2 reads of its board in a row |
| Closed jobs deleted after | `STORE_LIMITS.closedRetentionMs`, `referencedRetentionMs` | 7 days; 30 days for one a person saved or linked to an application (about 2% of board jobs close a day, measured 2026-11-10) |
| Feed jobs (Himalayas, Remote OK, Jobicy) close at | `STORE_LIMITS.feedMaxAgeMs` | their own end date, or 30 days after posting. Company boards' jobs have no age limit: open while the board lists them (owner decision 2026-11-10) |
| A Workday site is read again after | `INGEST_LIMITS.workdayRefreshAfterMs`, `workdayWorkers` | 44 hours, and at most 2 of the 4 workers read Workday sites while other boards wait. A read with nothing new is about 18 requests (robots.txt and 20 jobs a page), about 30 s at 1.5 s apart (8 of the biggest sites, measured 2026-11-22); each new post adds 1.5 s. The 147 sites posted about 19 jobs each a weekday into their newest 300 that week, so a read every other day is about 90 s, about 7,000 worker-seconds a day for all of them, close to what the 8 daily calls leave after the other boards: on busy weeks the oldest sites wait, so a site is read every 2 to 3 days. The first read of all 147 is about 44,000 posts (about 18 hours of one worker), so at this pace it takes about 10 days; `bun scripts/jobs-ingest-ci.ts --workers 12` against production does it in one sitting (about 2 hours) |
| A board is read again after | `INGEST_LIMITS.refreshAfterMs` | 20 hours. A full pass of all 718 boards is about 1,400 s of reads (605 took 1,100 s, measured from one laptop, 2026-11-10; the 85 Workable accounts take about 260 s of that at 3 s apart), so with 4 workers and the 240 s daily budget every board is read at least every 2 days |
| Openings one match request ranks | `CANDIDATE_POOL` | the best 300 by skill-weighted overlap, out of every open job (Free: 60, to show 5); pages after the 15th follow the match index's own order |
| Narrow table matching reads | `job_match` (`match-index.ts`) | one row per matchable opening, rebuilt after each ingest |
| A Matches page is reused for | `match_cache` | the day, until the next ingest, or until the resume, "Looking for", work profile or plan changes. A Pro ranking also keeps the next page, so Next reads the cache |
| Public `/jobs` and `/jobs/[id]` data | `public-cache.ts` | a day, dropped when the ingest finishes |
| Jobs-tab impressions kept | `RETENTION.impressionsMs` | 30 days (actions are kept) |
| Job title vectors | `title_vectors`, `TITLE_VECTOR_LIMITS`, `scripts/title-vectors.py` | One JobBERT-v2 vector per way a title is written (256 bytes, about 520 with its key and index), embedded once on GitHub's runner after the daily read, 15 rounds of 2,000 titles a day at most; a title no open job carries and nobody chose is dropped after 30 days (matching.md) |
| Hidden from Matches (still on `/jobs`) | `isFillerTitle`, `FRESH_FOR_MS` | "General application", "Talent community" and similar posts, and posts older than 90 days |

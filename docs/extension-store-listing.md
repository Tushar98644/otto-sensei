**Name:** Otto for Chrome

**Summary** (132 characters at most): See how your resume matches a job post, and whether you can take the job, right on LinkedIn, Naukri and company career pages.

## Description

No lists of job site or company names: the first version listed hiring systems by name and the store rejected it as keyword spam (2026-11-24).

Otto checks every job post against your resume, right on the page where you read it.

Open a job post on a job site or a company's careers page. A small Otto badge appears in the corner, with no click:

- The role and the company.
- How many of the post's skills your resume shows, such as "7 of 9 skills found", and the ones it's missing.
- Whether you can take the job, or a warning such as "Needs US work authorization".

Then one click:

- Save adds the job to your Otto board, with a copy of the post, because posts get taken down.
- Tailor opens Otto with a copy of your resume tailored to that exact job.
- Letter opens Otto writing a cover letter for it.

On any other site, press Otto's button in the toolbar and select the job post's text.

The match runs on your computer. Checking a post sends nothing anywhere; only Save sends the job to your Otto account.

On supported application forms, Fill with Otto fills in the form from your resume and the answers you saved, attaches your resume, and can draft answers to written questions. You check everything and press Submit yourself: Otto never sends an application and never answers self-identification or consent questions. When the company's page thanks you, Otto adds the application to your board.

The badge, the match, the work-rights check and Save are free. Tailor, Letter and Fill with Otto come with Otto Pro and Max.

Needs a free Otto Sensei account: the extension opens the connect page when you add it.

About the job

Contoso Pay builds the payments app small businesses across Asia run their day on. We're hiring a Senior Product Designer to lead checkout and onboarding, from first sketch to shipped pixels.

What you'll do

  • Own the design of checkout and onboarding across web, iOS and Android
  • Plan and run user research and usability testing with merchants
  • Grow our design system with engineers
  • Prototype new ideas fast and test them before they're built

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years designing consumer or fintech products
  • Expert in Figma and prototyping
  • Experience building and running a design system
  • Accessibility built into everything you ship
  • Enough HTML and CSS to pair with engineers

Nice to have

  • Framer
  • Motion design

Where you'll work

This role is fully remote within APAC. We hire in India, Singapore, Australia and the Philippines, and you'll overlap a few hours a day with our team in Singapore.

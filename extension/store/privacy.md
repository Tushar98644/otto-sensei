# Chrome Web Store privacy tab (draft)

## Single purpose

Otto for Chrome helps the person apply for jobs with their Otto Sensei account: it shows how their resume matches the job post they are reading and whether they can take the job, saves the post, and fills in that job's application form for them to check and send.

## Permissions

| Permission                                                                    | Why it's needed                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `storage`                                                                     | Keeps the account's connection key and the match data (resume text without contact details, work profile, saved job keys) on the device, so a job post is checked with no network request. |
| `activeTab`                                                                   | When the person presses the toolbar button on a site Otto doesn't support, lets Otto read that one tab, that one time.                                                                     |
| `scripting`                                                                   | With `activeTab`, injects the badge into the tab the person pressed the button on.                                                                                                         |
| Host: `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/*`                                       | Reads LinkedIn job posts (job pages and the job search pane only).                                                                                                                         |
| Host: `https://*.naukri.com/job-listings-*`                                   | Reads Naukri job listings.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Host: `https://*.indeed.com/*`                                                | Reads Indeed job posts and the search page's job pane; Indeed serves them on country domains.                                                                                              |
| Host: `boards.greenhouse.io`, `job-boards.greenhouse.io` (and their EU hosts) | Reads Greenhouse job boards, including the frame company sites embed, and fills their application forms when asked.                                                                        |
| Host: `jobs.lever.co`, `jobs.eu.lever.co`                                     | Reads Lever job posts and fills their application forms when asked.                                                                                                                        |
| Host: `jobs.ashbyhq.com`                                                      | Reads Ashby job posts and fills their application forms when asked.                                                                                                                        |
| Host: `https://*.myworkdayjobs.com/*`                                         | Reads Workday job posts and fills their application steps when asked; each company has its own subdomain.                                                                                  |
| Host: `jobs.smartrecruiters.com`, `apply.workable.com`                        | Reads SmartRecruiters and Workable job posts.                                                                                                                                              |
| Host: `https://www.ottosensei.com/*`                                          | Connects to the person's Otto account, fetches their match data, saves jobs, and fetches what Fill with Otto puts into a form.                                                             |

**Remote code:** none. Every script ships in the package.

## Data use

| Category                                                              | Collected?      | What and why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Personally identifiable information                                   | Yes             | The connected account's email, shown in the extension. When the person presses Fill with Otto: their name, email, phone, location, links, work history and education from their resume, and the answers they saved (notice period, pay, work rights, start date), fetched from Otto's server and put into that form; the extension doesn't keep them. |
| Authentication information                                            | Yes             | A random key for the person's Otto account, kept on the device; Otto's server keeps only its SHA-256.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Website content                                                       | Yes, on request | When the person presses Save, Tailor or Letter: the job post's link, title, company, location and text, sent to their Otto account. When they press Draft with Otto: that question and the post's text. After they send a form Otto filled: the job's link, title and company, to add the application to their board.                                 |
| Financial and payment information                                     | Yes, on request | The current and expected pay the person saves as answers, put into forms that ask for it. No payment details.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Health, personal communications, location, web history, user activity | No              | Otto reads job posts and their application forms only, and checking a post sends nothing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

**Certifications:** the data isn't sold to third parties, isn't used or transferred for purposes unrelated to the single purpose, and isn't used or transferred to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

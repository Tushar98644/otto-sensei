## Description

Otto checks every job post against your resume, on the page where you read it.

Open a job on LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, or a company's careers page on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters or Workable. A small Otto badge appears in the corner, with no click:

- The role and the company.
- How many of the post's skills your resume shows, such as "7 of 9 skills found", and the ones it's missing.
- Whether you can take the job: "Open to India (remote, APAC)", or a warning such as "Needs US work authorization".

Then one click:

- **Save** puts the job in To apply on your Otto board, with a copy of the post, because posts get taken down.
- **Tailor** opens Otto with a copy of your resume tailored to that exact job.
- **Letter** opens Otto writing a cover letter for it.

On any other site, press Otto's button in the toolbar: he reads the job post the page declares, or you select its text.

The match runs on your computer. Checking a post sends nothing anywhere; only Save sends the job to your Otto account.

Otto never fills in or sends an application for you, never touches LinkedIn's or Naukri's own Easy Apply, and doesn't read pages other than job posts.

The badge, the match, the work-rights check and Save are free. Tailor and Letter come with Otto Pro.

Needs a free Otto Sensei account: the extension opens the connect page when you add it.
